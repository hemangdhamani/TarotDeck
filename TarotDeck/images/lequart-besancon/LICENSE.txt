PUBLIC DOMAIN ARTWORK

All 78 card image files are faithful scans of a Lequart (Paris) tarot published in 1890. Each original Wikimedia Commons file page declares Public domain, Copyrighted=False and AttributionRequired=false; these values were checked individually at import and are recorded in SOURCE-MANIFEST.json with original URLs and content hashes. Commons identifies this as Tarot de Besançon - Arnoult. The download mirror labels the set Marseille, but the distinctive Juno and Jupiter trumps identify the Besançon pattern. Source collection: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Tarot_de_Besan%C3%A7on_-_Arnoult
