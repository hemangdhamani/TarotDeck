Woodcut Marseille tarot artwork by SONDLecT, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Source: https://github.com/SONDLecT/woodcut-tarot (commit e017dd5c8eb6991b53325f69c53acd9568d63201). Traced from Jean Noblet tarot (Paris, c. 1650, BnF RESERVE KH-34); Six through Ten of Wands use the contemporary Jacques Vieville deck to replace missing Noblet cards. BnF historical sources are linked in SOURCE-MANIFEST.json and SOURCE-README.txt. TarotDeck renders the full-color SVG paths as PNG and maps filenames to its canonical card IDs; captions and artwork remain as provided. Marseille numbering: Justice VIII, Strength XI. Offline readings use shared Rider-Waite-Smith-based meanings, not deck-specific historical divination meanings. CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
